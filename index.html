What the viewer is for

It shows all 19 sheets of the architect's 11/13/2026 set, with each revision item pinned where it applies. In it you can:

  • Pick any sheet; the count next to it is the number of open items on that sheet
  • Zoom and pan (mouse wheel or pinch), and fit the sheet to the screen
  • Click an item to jump to its pin, or click a pin to find its item
  • See which items come from DOB's emails (tagged DOB)
  • Tick items off as a personal worklist (saved only in your browser)
